Nibble

Three random photographs. Two are punched with same-size holes and stacked over the third. Click anywhere for a new combination.

Shapes

The shapes in the controls change the punch on the photos you’re looking at. Press [ and ] to step through them.

Download

Press D (or the arrow) to save the collage on screen as a high-quality JPG.

Favorites

Press F (or ♡) to favorite a combination; the exact holes and shape are kept, not just the photos. Press L (or the number beside it) to see your favorites.

Favorites live in this browser only. Use export / import in the list to back them up or move them.

Sources

Photos are drawn at random from Wikimedia Commons and belong to their authors under their own free licences.
